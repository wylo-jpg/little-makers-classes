課程簡介 About the Course

創科班（Little Makers Class）為期兩年，共 50 堂：中一（S1）25 堂打好基礎，中二（S2）25 堂深化進階。課程結合 STEAM 動手實作、編程思維、人工智能及專題研習，培養學生成為具創意與關愛精神的年輕創科人。

Little Makers Class is a two-year programme with 50 lessons: 25 foundation lessons in Form 1 (S1) and 25 advanced lessons in Form 2 (S2). It combines hands-on STEAM, computational thinking, artificial intelligence and project work to nurture creative and caring young makers.

全年主題 Annual Theme：用創科關愛弱勢社群
Using Innovation & Technology to Care for the Underprivileged
全年各單元均扣連此主題：由認識弱勢社群的需要出發，運用設計思維、STEAM 技能與 AI 工具，動手製作能真正幫助他人的作品。
