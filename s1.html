中一 S1課程 Form 1 Course · 25 Lessons

中一課程共 25 堂，分為 5 個單元。請選擇單元瀏覽各課堂教材。

The Form 1 course has 25 lessons in 5 units. Choose a unit to browse its lessons and materials.

單元 1：創科人出發
Unit 1: The Maker's Journey Begins · Lessons L01–L03

踏出創科第一步：建立工程師思維、掌握設計思維五步，並認識弱勢社群的生活，為全年「用創科關愛弱勢社群」主題奠基。

Take the first steps as a maker: build the engineer mindset, master the five steps of design thinking, and learn about the lives of the underprivileged — laying the foundation for the year's theme.

單元 2：動手做 STEAM
Unit 2: Hands-on STEAM · Lessons L04–L10

透過結構、能量、電磁、光影與電路等動手實驗學習科學原理，並完成關愛夜燈與發明家日記。

Learn science through hands-on experiments on structures, energy, electromagnetism, light and circuits — including a caring night light and an inventor's diary.

單元 3：編程思維
Unit 3: Computational Thinking · Lessons L11–L14

從演算法、二進制到排序搜尋與除錯，建立運算思維基礎。

Build computational-thinking foundations: algorithms, binary numbers, sorting, searching and debugging.

單元 4：AI 初探與 AI 倫理
Unit 4: Discovering AI & AI Ethics · Lessons L15–L20

初探人工智能：提示工程、AI 生成作品、圖像辨識，並深入探討 AI 倫理——真假資訊、偏見與私隱。

Discover AI — prompt engineering, AI-generated works, image recognition — and explore AI ethics: misinformation, bias and privacy.

單元 5：關愛 Project
Unit 5: The Caring Project · Lessons L21–L25

運用設計思維，由定題、設計到動手製作，完成一個關愛弱勢社群的專題，並公開展示成果。

Use design thinking to complete a caring project for the underprivileged — from topic selection and design to making — and present it at a showcase.
