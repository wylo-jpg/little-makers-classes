中二 S2課程 Form 2 Course · 25 Lessons

中二課程共 25 堂，分為 5 個單元。請選擇單元瀏覽各課堂教材。

The Form 2 course has 25 lessons in 5 units. Choose a unit to browse its lessons and materials.

單元 1：進階創科人
Unit 1: The Advanced Maker · Lessons L01–L03

以逆向工程拆解產品，更深入認識弱勢社群，由同情走向同理。

Take products apart through reverse engineering, understand the underprivileged more deeply, and move from sympathy to empathy.

單元 2：進階動手 STEAM
Unit 2: Advanced Hands-on STEAM · Lessons L04–L10

挑戰液壓機械臂、投石機、意粉橋、雞蛋保護裝置、進階電路與太陽能小車等進階動手任務。

Take on advanced hands-on challenges: hydraulic arm, trebuchet, spaghetti bridge, egg drop, circuits and a solar car.

單元 3：進階編程思維
Unit 3: Advanced Computational Thinking · Lessons L11–L14

流程圖、條件與迴圈、密碼學與演算法效率，深化編程思維。

Deepen computational thinking with flowcharts, conditions and loops, cryptography and algorithm efficiency.

單元 4：AI 進階與倫理深化
Unit 4: Advanced AI & Ethics · Lessons L15–L20

了解 AI 如何學習、體驗 AI 生成影片與音樂、進階提示技巧，並透過個案研討深化 AI 倫理思考，同時認識網絡安全。

Learn how AI learns, create AI video and music, master advanced prompting, deepen AI ethics through case studies, and explore cybersecurity.

單元 5：關愛 Project 進階
Unit 5: The Advanced Caring Project · Lessons L21–L25

走進社區實測方案，以進階設計與製作優化作品，最後向社區展示成果。

Test your solution in the community, refine it through advanced design and making, and showcase it to the community.
