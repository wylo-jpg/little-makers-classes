L11 流程圖追蹤器 Lesson 11: Flowchart Tracer

🧭 玩法 How to play：揀一個例子，按「開始追蹤」，睇住橙色光點沿住箭頭行。當去到菱形（判斷）時，由你決定行「是」定「否」——睇下唔同選擇會帶你去邊！
Press "Start trace" and watch the dot flow through the chart. When it reaches a decision diamond, YOU choose "Yes" or "No" and see where each path leads.
🍞 烘烤次數 Toasting rounds：0
⚠️ 已經烘烤咗好多次啦！再唔揀「是」，就會變成冇完冇了嘅迴圈（仲會燶埋添）！
Toasted many times already — keep choosing "No" and this becomes a never-ending loop (and burnt toast)!
按「開始追蹤」睇流程點行。
Press "Start trace" to follow the flow.
郁下上面嘅滑桿，再按「執行追蹤」——睇下輸入唔同，輸出點變！
Move the sliders, then run the trace — see how different inputs change the output!

符號圖例 Symbol legend — tap a symbol to find it in the chart

橢圓形 Oval開始／結束 Terminator
長方形 Rectangle處理 Process
菱形 Diamond判斷 Decision
平行四邊形輸入／輸出 Input/Output
箭頭 Arrow流程線 Flow line
📏 繪製流程圖三條規則 Three rules：
1. 箭頭只准單向，唔可以雙頭。Arrows point one way only.
2. 一個長方形只做一件事。One rectangle, one step.
3. 每個菱形必須有「是」同「否」兩條出路。Every decision needs both "yes" and "no" exits.
