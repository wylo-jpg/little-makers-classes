L11 互動模擬：機械人迷宮 Robot Maze — give precise, well-ordered instructions

玩法 How to play
  1. 用下面嘅按鈕加入指令（每步只有一個動作，同課堂一樣）。
    Add steps with the buttons below — one action per step.
  2. 按 ▶ 執行 睇機械人「照字面」執行你嘅指令；或用 ⏭ 逐步執行 逐行追蹤。
    Run it and watch the robot follow your instructions literally — or step through line by line.
