L13 密碼實驗室 Lesson 13: Cryptography Lab

🔐 玩法 How to play：「加密」分頁：打一句英文訊息，郁位移量滑桿（= 密鑰），即時睇密文，仲可以轉個密碼輪！「破解」分頁：扮做密碼破解員，用頻率分析或者逐個位移試，破解一段神秘密文。
Encrypt: type a message, move the shift slider (the key), and watch the cipher text change. Crack: play code-breaker — use frequency analysis or brute force on a mystery cipher text.

凱撒密碼加密 Caesar cipher — encryption

3
🎡 密碼輪點用：外圈係明文字母（唔郁）。郁位移量滑桿，內圈會轉動。編碼時：喺外圈搵明文字母，睇內圈同一個位置嘅字母，就係密文。
Outer ring = plaintext (fixed). Move the shift slider to rotate the inner disc. To encode: find the plaintext letter on the outer ring, read the inner letter at the same spot.

凱撒密碼解密 Caesar cipher — decryption

3
💡 解碼 = 將每個字母調返轉頭移位移量咁多位。Decoding shifts every letter backwards by the key.

🕵️ 破解挑戰 Code-breaking challenge

呢段密文用咗神秘位移量加密，你唔知密鑰！試下兩個破解方法：
This cipher text was encrypted with a secret shift. Crack it with two methods:

方法一：頻率分析 Method 1: frequency analysis

1. 數頻率 Count 2. 找最高 Find max 3. 推測位移 Guess shift 4. 驗證 Verify
按「第一步：數頻率」開始。
Press "Step 1: count frequencies" to begin.

方法二：暴力試誤 Method 2: brute force — try every shift

1
💡 凱撒密碼只有 25 種可能位移，逐個試好快就試完——所以佢好易被破解，唔安全！
Only 25 possible shifts exist — trying them all is quick, which is why the Caesar cipher is insecure!
