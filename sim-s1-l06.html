電磁鐵拾物比賽 Electromagnet Pick-up Contest — Interactive Simulation

玩法 How to play：調校線圈匝數同電池數目，按「通電」接上電池——看看電磁鐵吸起幾多枚回形針！再按「斷電」，睇下磁力點消失。
Set coil turns and battery count, then power on to attract paper clips. Power off and watch the magnetism vanish.

線圈 coil ＋ 鐵芯 iron core ＋ 電池 batteries ＝ 電磁鐵 electromagnet（電生磁！）

吸起回形針 Paper clips attracted：0 / 20 枚

安全提示（模擬裡面冇危險，真實做嗰陣）：嚴禁短路、唔好長時間接駁、用完即拆。
Real-world safety: never short-circuit, don't stay connected long, disconnect right after use.
