L01 逆向拆解實驗室 Teardown Lab: Reverse Engineering in Action

玩法 How to play
呢度有一架（已移除電池嘅）玩具車。請跟住逆向工程三個步驟：①拆解——按「由外而內」嘅次序撳車上嘅部件；②記錄——拆解記錄表會自動填好；③分析——挑戰設計意圖推測題，睇下你估唔估到設計師點諗！

A (battery-removed) toy car is waiting. Follow the three reverse-engineering steps: ① Disassemble — click the parts outside-in; ② Document — the record sheet fills itself; ③ Analyse — take the design-intention challenge.
