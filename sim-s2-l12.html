L12 條件與迴圈實驗室 Lesson 12: Conditions & Loops Lab

🧪 玩法 How to play：呢度有兩個實驗。第一個：較日子同埋時段，睇服藥提醒演算法點做決定。第二個：較「重複幾多次」，再試下刪走終止條件——睇下會發生咩事！
Two experiments: (1) change the day and time to see how the reminder algorithm decides; (2) set the repeat count — then try removing the termination condition and see what happens!
