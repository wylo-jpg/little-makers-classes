展示計時器 Presentation Timer

玩法 How to play：① 設定分鐘數（展示 3 分鐘、問答 1 分鐘）；② 按「開始」——大時鐘倒數，顏色由綠轉黃轉紅，時間到會響鈴；③ 跟著「1＋1＋1」階段提示講：每分鐘自動顯示該講什麼；④ 台下可用「互評速記」即時評分。

Set the minutes (3 for the showcase, 1 for Q&A), press start. The big clock counts down green → amber → red and chimes at the end. Follow the 1+1+1 stage prompts. The audience can score groups live with the quick peer-rating below.
