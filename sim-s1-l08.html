關愛夜燈電路 Glow Doorplate Circuit — Interactive Simulation

玩法 How to play：砌好門牌電路——放入電池、接好銅箔、接啱 LED 正負極。燈唔著？用下面的檢查清單逐步排解故障！
Build the circuit: insert the battery, close the foil loop, connect the LED with correct polarity. Lamp dark? Use the checklist to troubleshoot!

電路狀態 Circuit：？
🔧 燈唔著？逐步檢查 Lamp dark? Check step by step:
  1. 電池有冇放入？Is the battery in?
  2. 銅箔有冇完整接通？轉彎處有冇摺疊壓實？Is the foil loop complete? Corners pressed?
  3. LED 長腳（正極）係咪接電池正極？接反唔會著。Long leg (+) to battery (+)? Reversed won't light.

LED 單向導電：接反咗唔會燒壞，調轉再接就得。
LEDs conduct one way — reversed won't burn out, just flip it.
